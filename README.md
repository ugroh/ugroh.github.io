#### Über fünf Bücher von Segoe Kensaku 瀬越憲作

[Segoe Kensaku (1889–1972)](https://senseis.xmp.net/?Segoe) war ein Go-Spieler der japanischen [Taishō-](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taish%C5%8D-Zeit) und [Shōwa-](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dwa-Zeit) Zeitrechnung. Von 1947 bis 1948 war er Vorsitzender des Nihon Ki-in und trug wesentlich zur Popularisierung von Go in Japan bei. Aufgrund seiner sich verschlechternden Gesundheit und weil er vermeiden wollte, jemandem zur Last zu fallen, beging er im Jahr 1972 Selbstmord.

#### Segoe Kensaku »Lexikon des Tsume-Go 詰碁辞典«

In Ergänzung zu dem Beitrag DGoZ 02/2024 habe ich begonnen, das oben genannte Lexikon detaillierter darzustellen. In dieser Ergänzung ist auch erklärt, wo auf dem IZIS Go-Board das »Buch« versteckt ist.

In den genannten Verzeichnissen finden sich auch die Beiträge aus der DGoZ im PDF-Format, sofern diese bereits allgemein verfügbar sind.

#### About Five Books by Segoe Kensaku 瀬越憲作

[Segoe Kensaku (1889–1972)](https://senseis.xmp.net/?Segoe) was a Go player of the Japanese [Taishō](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taishō-Zeit) and [Shōwa](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shōwa-Zeit) eras. From 1947 to 1948 he was chairman of the Nihon Ki-in and contributed significantly to the popularization of Go in Japan. Due to his deteriorating health and because he wanted to avoid being a burden to anyone, he committed suicide in 1972.

#### Segoe Kensaku »Tsume-Go Dictionary 詰碁辞典«

In addition to the contribution DGoZ 02/2024, I have begun to present the above-mentioned dictionary in more detail. This supplement also explains where the »book« is hidden on the IZIS Go Board.

- In *Segoe-Kategorie.pdf* the 30 categories are discussed as they can be found in Segoe's book. For all these categories, the first ten problems can be found as exercises and solutions in *Segoe-TsumeGo.pdf*.
- For all those who would like to »read and solve« all of this interactively: *Segoe-TsumeGo.gobk* uses the [SmartGo program](https://smartgo.com/) and contains an overview of the categories and 10 problems each. There is also now an EPUB version that can also be used interactively with a suitable reader – such as the Apple Books app. All of this can be found in the subdirectory [Segoe-TsumeGo](https://github.com/ugroh/ugroh.github.io/tree/main/Segoe-TsumeGo).

The mentioned directories also contain the contributions from DGoZ in PDF format.

#### Additional Contributions

- *SGF* files of the games from Ueno Asami's »Play Like the Hammer Princess« can be found in the directory [UenoAsami](https://github.com/ugroh/ugroh.github.io/tree/main/UenoAsami). This article can be found [under this link.](https://github.com/ugroh/ugroh.github.io/tree/main/Beitrag-Gefuehl)
- *Contribution »Yose«* This article deals with finding the optimal way to play in the endgame. This is a contribution in *Gekkan Go Gaku* – 別冊 月刊碁学 Monthly Go School – of the Kansai Kiin, which appeared there regularly. This article can be found under this link.
- [100-AI-Joseki](https://github.com/ugroh/ugroh.github.io/tree/main/100-AI-Joseki) provides an overview of the 100 joseki in [the book of the same title](https://www.go-spiele.de/de/100-ai-joseki.html), as it does not contain an index or any other overview.
